01 — About

Built modern.
Run with heart.

On Hyderabad Road, beside the Tata Showroom, Bhooma Reddy Convention has been Nizamabad's go-to venue since 2016 — a contemporary glass building set behind a sweeping green lawn, with a fountain at its heart.

Inside: a grand hall that seats 2,000 guests, a separate banquet, 16 air-conditioned rooms and a kitchen that guests call the best in the city. No wonder it's rated 4.5★ by 935+ reviewers.

  • Grand hall — 2,000 seating / 3,000 floating
  • banquet hall — 250 seating & 1,000-dining capacity
  • Garden lawn with water fountains
  • 16 AC & non-AC rooms + private bridal suite
